Privacy Policy

DesktopFlow ERP by Awan Digital Solutions · Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DesktopFlow ERP, a product of Awan Digital Solutions (“we”, “us”, or “our”), may process information in connection with the software and related services.

DesktopFlow ERP is business management / ERP software. Where a customer enables the feature, DesktopFlow ERP may also integrate with the official Meta WhatsApp Business Platform to support business messaging.

This policy is intended to provide clear public information for users, customers, and platform reviewers. It does not create unsupported guarantees about security, compliance certifications, or absolute data practices.

2. Information DesktopFlow ERP may process

Depending on how the software is configured and used, DesktopFlow ERP may process:

  • Business records — organization profiles, settings, users, roles, and operational configuration.
  • Customer and supplier records — names, business details, addresses where entered, notes, and related relationship data.
  • Phone numbers and contact information — phone numbers, email addresses, and other contact details entered into the system.
  • Transaction, invoice, and receipt information — sales, purchases, invoices, receipts, payment-related records, and similar commercial data.
  • Account and authentication-related information — credentials and access controls managed by the customer’s deployment and authorized users.
  • Technical and operational logs — application events, error logs, and similar records needed to operate and support the software, where enabled.

3. WhatsApp Business Platform messaging data

If a customer enables WhatsApp Business Platform integration, DesktopFlow ERP may process messaging-related information necessary to send, receive, display, or track business messages. This may include, where applicable:

  • Phone numbers associated with WhatsApp conversations
  • Message content submitted through or displayed in the software
  • Message identifiers (message IDs)
  • Delivery, read, failure, or other status information returned by the platform
  • Template, conversation, or webhook-related metadata needed for messaging workflows

Messaging features that rely on Meta/WhatsApp are also subject to Meta’s and WhatsApp’s own terms, policies, and data practices. We do not control Meta’s independent processing of data on Meta’s systems.

4. How information is used

Information processed through DesktopFlow ERP is typically used to:

  • Provide and operate business management / ERP functionality
  • Create, store, and manage business, customer, supplier, and transaction records
  • Support communication workflows, including optional WhatsApp Business Platform messaging
  • Maintain account access, permissions, and system integrity
  • Provide support, troubleshoot issues, and improve reliability where applicable
  • Meet legal, accounting, tax, or regulatory obligations where required

5. Data storage and security

DesktopFlow ERP deployments may store data locally, on customer-controlled systems, or on infrastructure selected for a given installation. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Customers and users remain responsible for safeguarding credentials, devices, network access, and deployment configuration under their control.

6. Data retention

Information is retained for as long as needed to provide the service, support legitimate business operations, comply with legal or accounting requirements, resolve disputes, or enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type, customer configuration, and applicable law.

Business records such as invoices, receipts, and related transaction history may be retained longer where required for accounting, tax, audit, or legal purposes.

7. Third-party services

DesktopFlow ERP may interact with third-party services selected or enabled by the customer. Where WhatsApp Business Platform integration is used, Meta/WhatsApp may process messaging and related data according to their own policies and infrastructure.

This Privacy Policy does not replace the privacy notices of third-party providers. Customers and users should review applicable third-party terms and privacy policies before enabling such integrations.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared or disclosed only as needed to:

  • Operate the software and enabled integrations
  • Provide support at a customer’s request
  • Comply with law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request
  • Protect rights, safety, security, or integrity of users, customers, or the service

9. Data deletion requests

Users or customers may request deletion of data associated with their DesktopFlow ERP use. For instructions, see our Data Deletion Instructions.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legitimate business, legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution, or security purposes. Data processed independently by Meta/WhatsApp may also remain subject to Meta’s deletion mechanisms and retention practices.

10. Customer and user responsibilities

Customers who operate DesktopFlow ERP are responsible for ensuring they have an appropriate legal basis to collect and process business, customer, supplier, and messaging data entered into the system, and for configuring access controls appropriately for their organization.

11. Children’s privacy

DesktopFlow ERP is intended for business use. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly design the product for children’s use.

12. International considerations

Depending on deployment and enabled integrations, information may be processed in different locations, including by third-party platforms such as Meta. Customers should consider any applicable cross-border data requirements for their operations.

13. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product, legal, or operational changes. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published. Continued use of DesktopFlow ERP after an update constitutes notice that the revised policy applies, except where applicable law requires additional notice or consent.

14. Contact

For privacy or data-related questions about DesktopFlow ERP, contact Awan Digital Solutions through the communication channel already established for your account, support relationship, or commercial agreement.

Important: Do not send passwords, access tokens, App Secrets, webhook verify tokens, API keys, database credentials, or other secrets when making privacy inquiries.
