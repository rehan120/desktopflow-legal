About this site

This public website provides product information and the legal documents associated with DesktopFlow ERP, a product of Awan Digital Solutions.

DesktopFlow ERP is business management / ERP software. Where enabled by a customer, it may connect to Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform to support business messaging workflows.

  • Product DesktopFlow ERP — business management software for operational and commercial workflows.
  • Company Awan Digital Solutions develops and provides DesktopFlow ERP.
  • Optional messaging WhatsApp Business Platform integration is optional and, when used, is subject to applicable Meta/WhatsApp terms and policies.
