Data Deletion Instructions

DesktopFlow ERP by Awan Digital Solutions · Last updated: 25 September 2026

1. Purpose of this page

This page explains how a user or customer can request deletion of data associated with use of DesktopFlow ERP, a product of Awan Digital Solutions.

It is provided as a public reference for end users, business customers, and platform reviewers, including Meta app review requirements where applicable.

2. How to request deletion

To request deletion of data related to your DesktopFlow ERP account or business records, contact Awan Digital Solutions through the support, account, or commercial communication channel already established for your organization.

In your request, clearly state that you are requesting data deletion for DesktopFlow ERP.

3. Information needed to identify the relevant account

Please include enough information for us to locate the correct records, such as:

  • Full name of the requesting person
  • Business / organization name associated with DesktopFlow ERP
  • Account username or user identifier used in DesktopFlow ERP, if applicable
  • Registered phone number(s) or contact details linked to the account or WhatsApp messaging use, if relevant
  • Approximate location or branch name, if your organization operates multiple sites
  • A brief description of the data you want deleted
  • Your relationship to the account (for example: account owner, authorized administrator, or end user)

We may request additional verification to confirm that the requester is authorized to ask for deletion of the identified data.

Do not send secrets. Never include passwords, access tokens, Meta App Secrets, webhook verify tokens, API keys, database credentials, private keys, or other authentication secrets in a deletion request.

4. What happens after a request

After a valid deletion request is received and verified, Awan Digital Solutions will:

  1. Review the request and confirm the scope of data involved
  2. Verify the requester’s authority where needed
  3. Delete or anonymize eligible data associated with the identified DesktopFlow ERP use, subject to the exceptions below
  4. Provide confirmation when the request has been completed, or explain if certain data must be retained

Processing time may vary depending on the complexity of the request, the deployment model, and whether records are tied to legal or accounting retention requirements.

5. Retention exceptions

Some information may not be immediately deleted, or may be retained for a limited period, where retention is necessary for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Tax, accounting, or audit requirements
  • Fraud prevention, security, or abuse investigation
  • Dispute resolution or enforcement of agreements
  • Legitimate business records that a customer is required to keep

Where retention is required, access to retained information is limited to what is needed for those purposes.

6. WhatsApp / Meta data

If WhatsApp Business Platform integration was used, some messaging-related data may also exist on Meta/WhatsApp systems. Deletion of data held by Meta/WhatsApp is subject to Meta’s applicable deletion mechanisms, platform tools, and retention practices.

A deletion request to Awan Digital Solutions covers data under our or the customer’s DesktopFlow ERP control to the extent applicable. It does not automatically delete all copies of information that Meta or other independent third parties may process under their own systems and policies.

7. Customer-managed deployments

In deployments where a customer controls their own DesktopFlow ERP installation and data storage, the customer’s administrator may also need to delete or anonymize local records according to that organization’s internal procedures. Awan Digital Solutions can advise on product-related deletion steps where support access is available and authorized.

8. Related policies

For broader information about data practices, see the Privacy Policy. For service terms, see the Terms of Service.

9. Contact reminder

Submit deletion requests to Awan Digital Solutions using your existing support or account communication channel, and include the identifying details listed above.

Again: do not send passwords, access tokens, App Secrets, webhook verify tokens, API keys, or other credentials with your request.
